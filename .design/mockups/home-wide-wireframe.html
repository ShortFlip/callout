Wider Home, grey wireframe (1280×800)

  1. Hero band: full width, logo and wordmark larger than today's. This is the "bigger hero".
  2. Crew row: avatars of everyone you've played with. It's live content, and the first thing that makes the page about your group.
  3. Last night + champ: two tiles built from data we already have. Winner goes to History, champ goes to Leaderboard, which replaces today's two link cards.
  4. Rejoin: only shows when a room is live; otherwise the body moves up.
  5. Your Cards is the main panel: each card is drawn as a mini board, not a text row. Up to 3 fit without scrolling, and more go to Library.
  6. New card: opens Library with an empty card.
  7. Host rail: the largest button on the page. "Host this" on any card fills in the picker, so the flow is check card → Host without a dialog hunt.
  8. Join: stays in the rail and is secondary, since you're usually the host.
