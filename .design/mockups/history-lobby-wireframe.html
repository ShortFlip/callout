History, 1280×800

  1. Nights list: always visible on the left, and clicking a night swaps the right side instead of expanding in place. The newest night opens by default, or the one a ?night= link names (Game Over's Tonight's Results).

Lobby, 1280×800

  1. Left column: the room code stays big and readable aloud, with Copy Invite Link under it. Start sits at the bottom, with a line saying who you're still waiting on.
  2. Seats: your usual crew (everyone you've played with) as seats. They light up when someone joins and stay dimmed "Not here yet" otherwise. Anyone new who joins takes an open seat.
  3. Tonight's Card: the card as a miniature in its game colours, the same preview as Home.
  4. Rules: mode, board, win patterns, play-to. Read-only, so everyone knows the rules before Start.

Players see the same layout without Start; their bottom line reads "Waiting for the host to start".
