# 0005 — The 2026-10-04 design audit's rulings

- **Date:** 2026-10-05 (audit run 2026-10-04)
- **Symptom:** Squares was checked against his cross-app design rules
  (`~/.claude/docs/design-preferences.md`) and broke several: all-caps,
  letter-spaced captions ("ROOM CODE", "PLAYERS (4)"), panels at 14.4px and
  cards at 11.2px radius against his 6–10px range, an 11px uppercase header in
  the host's Swap dialog under the 13px floor, and lone spinners with the word
  "Loading…". Full report: `docs/design-audit-2026-10-04.md`.
- **Measurement:** Before/after screenshots of Home, Leaderboard, Lobby, the
  game screen, the Swap dialog and Game Over, both rendered against the local
  fake Supabase (`npm run dev:mock`), at 1440×900. He approved batch 1 from
  those on 2026-10-05.
- **Rule:**
  - Section captions are Title Case, 14px, semibold, normal tracking, from
    `SECTION_LABEL` in `src/lib/label.ts`. No uppercase or tracked captions.
  - Radius stops at 10: panels (`rounded-2xl`) 10, cards (`rounded-xl`,
    `rounded-lg`) 8, controls 6, chips 4. The cap lives in the `--radius-*`
    tokens in `globals.css`, so no class can exceed it.
  - A list that is loading shows `SkeletonRows` in the list's shape. A spinner
    is allowed only inside the button that started the work.
  - Removing a saved card acts at once and offers Undo on the toast
    (`restoreCard` flips saved back); the confirm dialog is gone, his call
    on 2026-10-05. Home's three rows share one 16px gutter. Status toasts
    are sentences ("Profile saved."); UI copy is US spelling ("canceled").
  - Mono only for the room and claim codes; every other number is the body
    face with `tabular-nums`, and pills are body 700 in Title Case ("Live",
    "1st"). One icon stroke, 1.75, set on `svg.lucide` in globals.css. A toast
    shows `error.message` only for a `LibraryError`, never a raw one.
  - Settled with him the same day, shipped in later batches: square text floor 11px; ground lifted; the marked-square
    glow trimmed to the dab; Latte keeps its logos visible.
- **Code site:** `src/lib/label.ts`, `src/components/ui/skeleton-rows.tsx`,
  `src/app/globals.css` (radius tokens), DESIGN.md "Type scale" and "Radius
  language".

The audit's fixes run as a punch list in `.design/AUDIT-PUNCHLIST.md`, four per
batch. Each later batch adds its rule here if it changes one.
